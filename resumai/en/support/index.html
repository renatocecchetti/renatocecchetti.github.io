Frequently asked questions

Why don’t I get a summary?

Summaries are made only by Apple Intelligence on Apple Private Cloud Compute. You need an iPhone 15 Pro or later, or an iPad with an M1 or A17 Pro chip or later, with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings > Apple Intelligence & Siri, and an internet connection. If Private Cloud Compute isn’t available or its usage limit was reached, the recording and transcript stay saved: open the record and tap Try Again later.

Is my audio sent to a server?

Not to ours — we don’t have servers. Recording and transcription happen on your device; the summary is made on Apple Private Cloud Compute, which doesn’t keep your content.

Which languages does it support?

The app is available in English, Spanish and Portuguese, and the summary follows the app language. You can record talks in any of these languages.

How do I remove ads?

App Settings > Remove Ads (one-time purchase). Already bought it? Use “Restore Purchases”.

How do I review my ad consent?

App Settings > Ad Privacy Options.

Recording stopped when I got a call.

The app pauses during system interruptions and resumes afterwards; check the recording indicator.
