An Otter, Notta and TurboScribe alternative on iPhone

If you are looking on iPhone for an alternative to cloud transcription services, here is ResumAI’s point: the app is built for classes, talks and meetings, it is free on the App Store, and we don’t operate servers with your audio.

Updated 25 November 2026

An honest comparison, and what this page is not

Otter, Notta and TurboScribe show up often when someone wants to transcribe a meeting, a lecture or an audio file. They are well-known products, generally built as transcription services — often in the company’s own cloud. This page is not a review of those apps: features, prices and policies change, and you should check each company’s site.

What I can state precisely is what ResumAI does, what it requires, and where it is (and is not) an alternative on iPhone.

What ResumAI is

ResumAI is an iPhone and iPad app that captures the audio, slide photos and notes from a talk, class or meeting and returns a one-page PDF. If the device supports Apple Intelligence, the PDF includes a central idea, topics, insights and quotes, plus the transcript and photos. Without Apple Intelligence, the app still records, transcribes and assembles a PDF with the transcript and slides.

We don’t have servers that hold that content. Processing happens on the device or on Apple Private Cloud Compute. Version 1.2, already on the App Store, adds Apple Pencil on iPad: you can draw and annotate right on the summary PDF, and your marks are included in the exported file.

Where it can make sense as an alternative

  • You use an iPhone or iPad and want to leave a class, talk or conference with a briefing in PDF, not just an audio file.
  • You would rather not send the audio of a class or meeting to a server of ours — because we don’t run one.
  • You want a free App Store app (with ads), without creating an account with us.

What ResumAI does not promise

  • It does not run on Android, Windows or in a browser.
  • It is not a bot that joins Zoom, Meet or Teams on its own.
  • It is not a legal transcript, a medical record or a perfect transcription: names, acronyms and technical terms can come out wrong.
  • The full summary needs Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro or later, or an iPad with M1/A17 Pro or later, with Apple Intelligence turned on and, when the device uses Apple Private Cloud Compute, an internet connection). The app itself requires iOS or iPadOS 27 or later.
  • Privacy in the app does not replace consent: record only if the class, talk or meeting allows it.

Frequently asked questions

Does ResumAI send audio to its own servers?

No. We don’t operate servers that hold your recordings. Audio, transcripts, slide photos and summaries are processed on your iPhone or iPad, or on Apple Private Cloud Compute.

Is ResumAI a replacement for Otter, Notta or TurboScribe?

Not exactly. They are different products. ResumAI is an iPhone and iPad app for recording a talk, a class or a meeting, photographing slides and returning a PDF — with the content on your device or on Apple Private Cloud Compute. It is not a videoconference bot, and it is not a third-party cloud transcription service. Check each company’s site for what they offer today.

Is the app free?

Yes. ResumAI is free on the App Store and shows ads. The one-time “Remove Ads” purchase turns off ads and ad tracking. Ads do not use the content of your talk or class.

Does it work on Android or on a computer?

No. ResumAI is an iPhone and iPad app, and it requires iOS or iPadOS 27 or later. The full summary with Apple Intelligence needs a compatible device; without it, the app still records, transcribes and builds a PDF with the transcript and slides.
