A private talk or meeting summary on iPhone

You can record a talk or a meeting and leave with a PDF without handing the audio to a service of ours. It helps to know what stays on the device, what may go to Apple Private Cloud Compute, and what that does not authorize you to do.

Updated 25 November 2026

What “private” means in ResumAI

We do not have servers holding your recordings. Audio, transcripts, slide photos and summaries are processed on your iPhone or iPad, or on Apple Private Cloud Compute. Apple describes that cloud as an environment that does not keep your content. The full summary with Apple Intelligence may use that path; transcription and the PDF with slides still happen on the device when Apple Intelligence is not available.

That is different from “everything always happens 100% offline.” Recording itself does not require an account with us or an upload to us. A network connection may be needed for a summary via Private Cloud Compute, when the device uses that feature. If you are offline, you can still record; the full summary waits until the device can finish it.

Confidential meetings and talks

In-app privacy does not replace consent. In a closed meeting, ask before you record. At a talk, follow the event’s rule: many organizers allow personal notes and forbid republishing the audio or the PDF. The file living on your device does not authorize you to forward it to people who were not in the room.

Ads in the free version (Google AdMob) may use device identifiers and ad-interaction data — never the content of the talk or meeting. The one-time “Remove Ads” purchase turns off ads and ad tracking.

A simple, discreet method

  1. One record per meeting or talk. Do not mix agendas.
  2. Phone on silent, microphone clear, face down if you want fewer distractions.
  3. Photograph only what you will not remember: a chart, an agreement on the board, the agenda.
  4. When it ends, generate the PDF and reread it the same day. Fix names and figures while the session is still fresh.
  5. Export only what you need to keep elsewhere. There is no account of ours with the history.

Apple Pencil, if you are on iPad

ResumAI 1.2 adds Apple Pencil support on iPad: you can draw and annotate right on the summary PDF, and your marks are included in the exported PDF. Useful for circling a sensitive point or an action item without rewriting the whole page.
