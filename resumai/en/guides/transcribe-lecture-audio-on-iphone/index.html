How to transcribe lecture audio on iPhone

Your iPhone can already record a class. The useful next step is leaving with the text — and, if the device allows it, a summary — without sending the audio to our servers.

Updated 25 November 2026

Why transcribe, not just record

A 50-minute audio file is hard to review. A transcript lets you search a term, jump back to a definition and paste a passage into your notes. In ResumAI, transcription happens on the iPhone or iPad itself. That still works when Apple Intelligence is not available: the app records, transcribes and builds a PDF with the transcript and your slide photos.

Before you start

Check whether the class allows recording. In many schools personal notes are fine and publishing the audio is not. Ask the instructor if it is unclear. Place the phone face down, microphone unobstructed, and silence notifications. In a large room, sitting closer to the front usually helps recognition.

The steps in ResumAI

  1. Open a new record at the start of class — one record per lecture, so subjects do not mix.
  2. Let the recording run. When the instructor writes on the board or shows an important slide, take a photo.
  3. If a name, a formula or a reading list comes up, jot it in one line. The transcript captures speech; it does not read the board on its own.
  4. When class ends, tap Finish. The PDF includes the transcript and the photos. On a device with Apple Intelligence, the summary (core idea, topics, insights and quotes) is added to the same file.

What the transcript is not

It is not a legal record and it is not perfect. Proper names, acronyms and technical terms can come out wrong, especially with accent, echo or chatter in the next row. It is worth rereading the PDF the same day and fixing what matters. ResumAI also does not replace being in class: it organizes what was said; it does not invent what you missed by not paying attention.

If you are on iPad

ResumAI 1.2 adds Apple Pencil support on iPad: after the PDF is generated, you can draw and annotate right on the summary, and your marks are included in the exported file. Details are in Apple Pencil notes at a talk.

Privacy, in one sentence

Recordings, transcripts, photos and summaries are processed on your device or on Apple Private Cloud Compute. None of that goes to our servers. Ads in the free version do not use the content of the lecture.
