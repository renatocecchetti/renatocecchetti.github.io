How to summarize a sermon or service on iPhone

If the community allows recording, you can leave the service with a PDF to reread during the week — a transcript and, when the device allows it, a summary. The content stays on your iPhone or iPad, or goes only to Apple Private Cloud Compute.

Updated 25 November 2026

A personal use, with respect for the community

Many people want to take home the thread of a sermon, a Bible study or a teaching service — not to republish it, but to reread it during the week. ResumAI can record the audio, take a note you add and return a PDF. If the device has Apple Intelligence, the file includes a central idea, topics, insights and quotes, plus the transcript. Without it, you still leave with the transcript.

That use only makes sense under the community’s rule. Some churches record and publish the sermon; others ask that the moment stay in the room. Ask if it is not obvious.

A discreet way to record

  1. One recording per service or study, with the date in a note if you want to find it later.
  2. Device on silent, screen down, microphone clear. Avoid filming the people around you.
  3. If there is a verse, an outline or a board, a photo can help — provided the community allows photographs.
  4. When it ends, generate the PDF and reread it the same day. The transcript can get names, quotations and theological terms wrong; correct what matters.
  5. On iPad, version 1.2 lets you annotate the PDF with Apple Pencil; the marks are included in the exported file.

What the app does not do

It does not publish the sermon, it does not send the audio to a server of ours, and it does not stand in for being present. It is also not an official transcript for the church archive. Ads in the free version do not use the content of the service. The one-time “Remove Ads” purchase turns ads off.

Frequently asked questions

Can I record a service or sermon without asking?

Not automatically. Many communities allow personal notes and ask that the audio and the record not be published. Ask the leadership if the rule is not clear. Privacy in the app does not replace the community’s consent.

Does ResumAI publish the sermon or send the audio to your cloud?

No. We don’t have servers that hold your recordings, and the app does not publish anything on your behalf. Processing happens on the device or on Apple Private Cloud Compute. You decide what to export — and you should respect what the community allows.

Does it count as an official church record?

No. The PDF is personal study material: a transcript, photos (if you take them) and, when Apple Intelligence is available, a summary with a central idea, topics, insights and quotes. It is not minutes, an official bulletin or a legal transcript.

Does it help if I only want to study what was preached?

Yes, that is the intended use: record, when it is allowed, and reread the PDF the same day. On iPad, version 1.2 lets you annotate the summary with Apple Pencil, and the marks are included in the exported PDF.
