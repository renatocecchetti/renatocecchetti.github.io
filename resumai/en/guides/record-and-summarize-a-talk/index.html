How to record and summarize a talk on iPhone

A practical walkthrough for capturing audio, slides and your own notes, then leaving with a one-page PDF — without sending that content to our servers.

Updated 24 November 2026

Before you leave

  • Check that the iPhone or iPad is on iOS or iPadOS 27 or later. The full summary uses Apple Intelligence; without it, ResumAI still records, transcribes and builds a PDF with the transcript and slides.
  • Leave enough storage and battery. A one-hour talk makes a large audio file. Bring a charger if you can.
  • Switch the device to silent, and silence low-battery alerts if they tend to chime mid-sentence.
  • Open ResumAI once at home and grant the microphone permission, so you are not doing that in a hallway.

Only record when the event allows it

Events set their own rules. Some ban recording; others allow audio for personal notes but not publication. Check the site, the badge or the announcement at the start. If the speaker asks you to pause, pause.

ResumAI is built for personal notes: audio and summaries stay on your device (the summary may run on Apple Private Cloud Compute). That is not the same as permission to publish the recording, the transcript or the PDF. If you are unsure, keep the file for your own study and do not share it.

During the talk

  1. Open ResumAI and start a new record before the talk begins. Sit close enough for a clear voice — the app will still record from farther away, but transcription suffers.
  2. Keep the device steady, with the microphone unobstructed. You do not need to point the camera the whole time.
  3. Photograph the slide when it changes. You do not need every slide: prioritize charts, definitions and the talk’s outline. Photos stay in the same record.
  4. Jot down what a transcript will miss: a project name, a question you want to ask, a “look this up later.” A few words are enough.
  5. If a call or an alarm interrupts, check that recording has resumed. ResumAI pauses for system interruptions and usually continues afterwards.

When you finish

Tap Finish and wait for the PDF. The one-page brief collects the central idea, topics, insights and quotes. It is an organized draft, not a certified transcript: reread it and fix a name or a figure before you share it.

Your content is processed on the iPhone or iPad, or on Apple Private Cloud Compute. We do not have servers holding your recordings, transcripts, photos or summaries.

If the summary does not appear

Check that Apple Intelligence is on in Settings. Without it, you still keep the audio, the transcript and the slides in the PDF. More detail is on the support page.

Get ResumAI

The app is free on the App Store for iPhone and iPad, and it shows ads. The one-time “Remove Ads” purchase turns them off. Ads do not use the content of your talks.
