Guide

Lecture summaries with on-device AI, without sending audio to our cloud

You can leave a lecture with a structured PDF and keep the audio on your device. This guide explains what ResumAI does, what it does not do, and when a school or instructor may not allow recording.

Updated 25 November 2026

What “not sent to the cloud” means here

ResumAI does not run servers that hold the content of your classes. Recordings, transcripts, photos of a whiteboard or slides, and summaries are processed on the iPhone or iPad, or on Apple Private Cloud Compute. They do not go to a cloud of ours, and we do not use that content to train a model of ours.

That is different from an app that uploads the audio to the company’s servers and sends the text back. It is also different from “nothing ever leaves the device”: when the summary uses Apple Private Cloud Compute, the necessary portion is processed on Apple’s infrastructure, under Apple’s rules. The details are in the Privacy Policy.

The free version shows Google AdMob ads. Those ads may use device identifiers and ad-interaction data — not the content of the lecture. The “Remove Ads” purchase turns off the ads and the associated tracking.

How to use it in a class

  1. Confirm that the institution and the instructor allow recording for personal study. Many classrooms do not. If recording is not allowed, do not use the app.
  2. Start the record at the beginning of the class, not in the middle of an argument.
  3. Photograph the board or the slides when a definition, a formula or a diagram appears that you will want in the PDF.
  4. Note the textbook page, the exercise number or the question you want to ask at the end. A transcript almost never recovers those on its own.
  5. When class ends, generate the PDF and reread it the same day. The summary is a study aid, not a substitute for attending or for doing the exercises.

Honest limits

  • The full summary needs a device that supports Apple Intelligence, with the feature turned on. Without that, the app still delivers a transcript and slides.
  • The minimum system is iOS or iPadOS 27.
  • In a noisy room, transcription will get names, acronyms and numbers from the board wrong. That is why photos and your short notes matter.
  • Do not publish or circulate the PDF if the class may not be recorded, or if the instructor has not allowed sharing.

Studying afterwards

Keep the PDF on the device and use it to recall the central idea and the questions you wrote down. If the material will be on an exam, treat the summary as a draft: check definitions and formulas against the course materials.

The same flow works for an open lecture or a seminar. The recording walkthrough is in How to record and summarize a talk on iPhone.

Apple Pencil on iPad (version 1.2)

On iPad, ResumAI 1.2 adds Apple Pencil support: you can draw and annotate right on the summary PDF, and your marks are included in the exported PDF. That comes after the PDF is generated — during the talk, the essentials are still the audio, the slide photos and a short note. There is a dedicated guide: Apple Pencil notes at a talk.

Get ResumAI

The app is free on the App Store for iPhone and iPad, and it shows ads. The one-time “Remove Ads” purchase turns them off. Ads do not use the content of your talks.
