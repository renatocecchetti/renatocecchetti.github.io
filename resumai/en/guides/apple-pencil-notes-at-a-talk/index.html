Apple Pencil: how to annotate a talk on iPad

ResumAI 1.2 adds Apple Pencil support on iPad. You can draw and annotate right on the summary PDF, and your marks are included in the exported PDF. This guide covers when to use the pencil — and what it does not replace.

Updated 25 November 2026

What version 1.2 does, precisely

On iPad, you can draw and annotate right on the summary PDF. Those marks become part of the file when you export it. The feature is for iPad with Apple Pencil; on iPhone the flow is still record, photograph slides, type a short note and generate the PDF.

Pencil annotations come after the summary exists. During the talk, the essentials are still the microphone, the slide photos and, if you want, a short typed note. When the session ends, tap Finish, open the PDF, and then use the pencil.

A practical way to use it

  1. Record the talk on iPad (or on iPhone and continue on iPad, if the record is already on that device).
  2. Generate the PDF. If Apple Intelligence is available, you get the core idea, topics, insights and quotes; without it, the transcript and slides.
  3. With Apple Pencil, underline the main argument, circle a figure, or connect a quote to a margin note of your own.
  4. Export the PDF when you want to take those marks into another app or share them with a colleague — always within the event’s rules.

What the pencil does not do

It does not transcribe the whiteboard for you, it does not automatically fix a wrong name in the transcript, and it does not authorize recording an event that forbids recording. This page is also not a catalog of every compatible Pencil model: use an Apple Pencil your iPad already recognizes for marking up PDFs.

Conference, class or meeting

At a conference with several sessions, finish each one before the next and mark only what you will revisit in the break. In class, the pencil helps tie the summary to a problem set. In a meeting, an arrow next to an action item can replace rewriting the minutes. The content stays on your device, or goes only to Apple Private Cloud Compute when the summary is processed — not to our servers.
