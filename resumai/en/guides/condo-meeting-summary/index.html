A condo or homeowners’ meeting summary on iPhone

You can leave the meeting with a PDF to reread decisions and deadlines — if the meeting allows recording. ResumAI does not replace the official minutes. Content is processed on the device or on Apple Private Cloud Compute.

Updated 25 November 2026

A personal briefing, not the official minutes

Building and homeowners’ meetings run long, mix accounts, works and debate, and the formal minutes often arrive later. ResumAI can help you leave with a PDF to check what was said — a transcript, a photo of the board or the agenda, and, if the device has Apple Intelligence, a central idea, topics, insights and quotes. That does not replace the minutes, the attendance book or the managing agent.

Consent first

Say that you intend to record, and go ahead only if there is permission. If the meeting refuses, use handwritten notes. Forwarding the audio or the PDF to the residents’ group, without agreement, is a separate decision — and the app does not authorize it for you. We don’t have servers with that content; processing is on the device or on Apple Private Cloud Compute. That is processing privacy, not a license to distribute.

A simple method

  1. One recording per meeting, with the date and the type (ordinary, special) in a note.
  2. Device on silent, microphone clear. Photograph the agenda, a board of figures or the result of a vote — not documents with unnecessary third-party data.
  3. When it ends, generate the PDF and reread it the same day. Correct amounts, names and deadlines. The transcript will get numbers and surnames wrong.
  4. On iPad, annotate the PDF with Apple Pencil if you want to highlight a decision; the marks are included in the exported file.
  5. Keep the file for personal use, unless the meeting authorizes another destination.

Frequently asked questions

Does the ResumAI PDF replace the official minutes?

No. The official minutes remain the document of the board, the building manager or whoever the bylaws name. ResumAI returns a personal briefing — a transcript and, when available, a summary — so you can follow what was said. Do not treat the file as legal proof without the proper process.

Do I need to say that I am recording?

Yes, as a rule. A condo or homeowners’ meeting involves neighbors and, often, money and conflict. Ask for permission before recording and follow what the assembly or the bylaws require. The audio staying on your device does not authorize you to forward it to the building group or to third parties.

Does the audio go to a server of yours?

No. We don’t have servers that hold your recordings. Processing happens on the iPhone or iPad, or on Apple Private Cloud Compute. Ads in the free version do not use the content of the meeting.

Can I mark decisions on the PDF?

On iPad, version 1.2 lets you draw and annotate right on the summary PDF, and the marks are included in the exported file. An arrow next to a vote or a deadline may be enough, without rewriting the text.
