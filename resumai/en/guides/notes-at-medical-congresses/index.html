How to take notes at medical congresses on iPhone

At a congress with many sessions, the useful outcome is leaving each room with a PDF — and recording only when the event allows it. ResumAI organizes audio, slides and notes on iPhone or iPad, without servers of ours.

Updated 25 November 2026

A medical congress is a special case

There are several sessions in one day, slides that move quickly, and, often, clinical content that should not circulate. ResumAI can help you leave each room with an organized PDF — a transcript, photos of the slides and, if the device has Apple Intelligence, a summary with a central idea, topics, insights and quotes. It does not replace the event’s rules or your professional judgment.

Before you hit record

Read the regulations and whatever is posted at the door. If recording is not allowed, use only what is authorized: handwritten notes, photos of public slides, or nothing. In panels that discuss patient cases, the default should be the most restrictive. Privacy in the app is not the same as permission to record or forward.

A method for a day full of sessions

  1. One recording per session, with the room or topic in the title or in an opening note. Don’t mix the morning panel with the afternoon symposium.
  2. Device on silent, microphone clear. Photograph the slide you will not remember — a diagram, a reference, a number — never identifiable patient data.
  3. When the session ends, tap Finish before the next one starts. Reread the PDF during the break and correct drug names, acronyms and figures while the talk is still fresh.
  4. On iPad, version 1.2 lets you annotate the PDF with Apple Pencil; the marks are included in the exported file. Export only what you have a right to keep.

Honest limits

The transcript will get technical terms wrong. The summary may omit a clinical detail that, for you, was the detail. Cross-check with the proceedings, the official materials and your own notes. And don’t treat the PDF as a medical document or proof of attendance.

Frequently asked questions

Can I record every session at a medical congress?

No. Follow the event rules and the notice in each room. Many congresses allow personal notes and forbid recording, filming or republishing the audio and the materials. If a session discusses a clinical case, the duty of care is even higher: the file staying on your device does not authorize you to share it.

Does ResumAI produce a medical record or a certificate?

No. The app returns a briefing PDF — a transcript and, when Apple Intelligence is available, a central idea, topics, insights and quotes — plus photos of the slides. It is not a medical record, a certificate, continuing-education credit or the official minutes of the congress.

Does the session content leave my iPhone?

It does not go to servers of ours. Processing happens on the device or on Apple Private Cloud Compute. Ads in the free version do not use the content of the session.

Can I use Apple Pencil between sessions?

On iPad, version 1.2 lets you draw and annotate right on the summary PDF, and your marks are included in the exported PDF. The practical flow is to finish each session, generate the PDF and mark only what you will come back to during the break.
