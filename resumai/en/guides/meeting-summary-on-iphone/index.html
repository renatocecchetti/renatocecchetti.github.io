How to summarize a meeting on iPhone

A one-page PDF to reread decisions and deadlines after an in-person meeting. Content is processed on the device or on Apple Private Cloud Compute — not on servers of ours. Tell people before you record.

Updated 27 November 2026

Who it is for

For anyone leaving an in-person meeting — with a team, a client, a supplier or a committee — who wants a short record to reread, without sending the audio to yet another cloud service. ResumAI records the audio, takes photos of the whiteboard or slides and your notes, and returns a one-page PDF. With Apple Intelligence, the PDF has a central idea, topics, insights and quotes, plus the transcript and the photos.

Where the content stays

We don’t have servers that hold the recordings. Content is processed on the device or on Apple Private Cloud Compute, which Apple describes as an environment that does not keep your content. That helps when the agenda is sensitive, but it does not replace company policy or telling the participants.

A five-step method

  1. Before you start, say you will record and confirm you may. If not, use notes and whiteboard photos only.
  2. One recording per meeting, with the subject and date in a note.
  3. Photograph what audio does not hold well: a board of figures, a timeline, the list of owners.
  4. When it ends, generate the PDF and reread it within ten minutes. Fix names, amounts and dates — the transcript gets surnames and numbers wrong.
  5. On iPad, version 1.2 lets you mark the PDF with Apple Pencil; an arrow next to a decision already helps the follow-up. The marks are included in the exported file.

Frequently asked questions

Does the meeting audio go to a server of yours?

No. We don’t have servers that hold your recordings. Processing happens on the iPhone or iPad, or on Apple Private Cloud Compute. Ads in the free version do not use the content of the meeting.

Do I need to say that I am recording?

Yes. Tell the participants and follow your company’s policy. Some companies forbid recording internal or client meetings. Processing privacy does not replace the consent of the people speaking.

Does the PDF replace the official minutes or notes?

No. ResumAI returns a one-page personal briefing: a transcript, photos of the whiteboard or slides and, with Apple Intelligence, a central idea, topics, insights and quotes. Check decisions, deadlines and owners before you share anything.

What if Apple Intelligence is not available?

The app still records, transcribes and builds a PDF with the transcript and the photos. The full summary needs iOS or iPadOS 27 or later on an Apple Intelligence device (iPhone 15 Pro or later, or an iPad with M1/A17 Pro or later).
