How to study for an exam with a class recording

A prep-course recording only pays off if you come back to it the same day. A PDF with the transcript, slides and, when possible, a summary shortens the review — on iPhone or iPad, with the content on your device.

Updated 25 November 2026

A recording only helps with a method

In exam prep, the temptation is to pile up hours of audio and leave it until the night before. That usually fails. ResumAI works better like this: you attend the class, capture the audio and the slides, and the same day turn that into a review PDF. If the device has Apple Intelligence, the file includes a central idea, topics, insights and quotes, plus the transcript and photos. Without it, you still leave with the transcript and slides.

A review flow

  1. One recording per class, with the subject and the date in the title or in a note.
  2. Photograph what audio does not hold well: a diagram on the board, a table, the list of what will be on the test.
  3. When you leave, tap Finish. Reread the PDF in ten minutes. Correct a legal term, a formula, a cited holding.
  4. In the weekly review, start with the PDF, not the audio. Go back to the recorded passage only when a point is unclear.
  5. On iPad, use Apple Pencil to mark what tends to appear on the exam; the annotations are included in the exported PDF, in version 1.2.

Honest limits

The transcript will get jargon, statute numbers and authors’ names wrong. The summary is not an official map of the syllabus. Cross-check with the course materials and with practice questions. And record only if the class allows it. Content is processed on the device or on Apple Private Cloud Compute — not on servers of ours. Ads in the free version do not use the content of the class.

Frequently asked questions

Can I record a prep course or university lecture?

Only if the class allows it. Many prep courses and universities allow personal notes and restrict recording or publication. Ask. Publishing the audio or the PDF is usually a separate matter.

Does the summary replace the class and the exercise list?

No. ResumAI organizes what was said. It does not attend class for you, and it may omit the detail that appears on the exam. Start the review with the PDF, go back to the audio only at the unclear point, and cross-check with the teacher’s materials.

What if Apple Intelligence is not available?

The app still records, transcribes and generates a PDF with the transcript and photos of the slides. The summary with a central idea, topics, insights and quotes needs a device compatible with Apple Intelligence, and iOS or iPadOS 27 or later.

How does Apple Pencil fit into this study?

On iPad, version 1.2 lets you draw and annotate right on the summary PDF, and the marks are included in the exported file. You can mark what tends to appear on the exam and take those marks into the review.
