The pitch, in brief

ResumAI is a Brazilian iPhone and iPad app that turns the audio, slide photos and notes from a talk, class or meeting into a one-page PDF. We don’t operate servers that hold that content: processing happens on the device or on Apple Private Cloud Compute.

The app is free on the App Store and shows ads (ads do not use the content of the talk). There is a one-time “Remove Ads” purchase. Version 1.2, already live, adds Apple Pencil on iPad: you can draw and annotate right on the summary PDF, and your marks are included in the exported PDF.

Price
Free, with Google AdMob ads. The one-time “Remove Ads” purchase turns off ads and ad tracking.
What it does
Records audio, takes slide photos and notes, and returns a one-page PDF. With Apple Intelligence: a central idea, topics, insights and quotes, plus the transcript and photos. Without Apple Intelligence: a PDF with the transcript and slides.
Privacy
We don’t have servers that hold the recordings. Content is processed on the device or on Apple Private Cloud Compute, which Apple describes as an environment that does not keep your content.
Requirements
iOS or iPadOS 27 or later. The full summary needs Apple Intelligence: an iPhone 15 Pro or later, or an iPad with M1/A17 Pro or later, with Apple Intelligence turned on and, when the device uses Apple Private Cloud Compute, an internet connection.
What’s new in 1.2
Apple Pencil on iPad: drawing and annotations right on the summary PDF; marks are included in the exported PDF.
