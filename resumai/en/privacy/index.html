Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy explains how the ResumAI app (“App”) handles personal data. It is written under Brazil’s General Data Protection Law (Law No. 13,709/2018 — LGPD) and, for users in the European Economic Area and the UK, the GDPR. Controller: Renato Cassola, individual, contact renatocecchetti@gmail.com (also the data protection contact).

1. Your content never comes to us

Audio recordings, transcripts, slide photos, recognized text, notes and summaries (“Your Content”) are stored only on your device. Transcription runs on your device. Summaries are written by Apple Intelligence on Apple Private Cloud Compute, under Apple’s privacy rules: Apple states that data sent to Private Cloud Compute is used only to fulfill the request and is not stored. The developer runs no servers and does not receive, store or have access to Your Content. You share Your Content only when you choose to (for example, through the iOS share sheet).

2. Data handled for ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows Google AdMob ads. Google’s SDK may collect: device identifiers (including the advertising identifier, IDFA, only if you allow tracking in the iOS prompt), ad interaction and usage data, approximate location derived from the IP address, and diagnostic data (crashes and performance). Purposes: showing and measuring ads, preventing fraud and, with your consent, personalizing ads. Legal bases: consent for tracking and personalization, and legitimate interest for non-personalized ads, fraud prevention and security. Where required (for example in the EEA and the UK), the App asks for your consent through Google’s consent form before ads are personalized. Google processes this data as an independent controller under the Google Privacy Policy, and it may be transferred internationally. The developer does not receive this data in a way that identifies you.

3. Purchases

The “Remove Ads” purchase is processed by Apple. The App only checks, on your device, whether the purchase exists; we do not receive your payment details. After the purchase, ads and the ads SDK are no longer used to show ads, which ends ad tracking by the App.

4. Permissions

Microphone (record the talk), speech recognition (transcribe on device), camera and photos (slides), notifications (tell you when the summary is ready) and tracking (personalized ads, optional). You can change them at any time in iOS Settings. In the App’s Settings, “Ad Privacy Options” lets you review your ad consent.

5. Retention and deletion

Your Content stays on your device until you delete the record or the App. Ad data follows Google’s retention rules.

6. Your rights

You can ask for confirmation, access, correction, anonymization, portability, deletion, information about sharing, objection and withdrawal of consent by writing to the email above. Because we do not store Your Content, most of these rights are exercised directly on your device. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Brazil, the ANPD).

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

Changes to this policy will be published on this page with a new date.
